Before you start.

Fynder is currently a Windows desktop prototype. Keyword search works without an AI model.

To search by meaning, install Ollama, download nomic-embed-text, enable local AI, and refresh the index.

Visit the developer on GitHub ↗
What can I search?

PDF, DOCX, XLSX, TXT, MD, CSV, JSON, LOG, HTML and HTM files. Files over 30 MB are skipped. Image-only PDFs require OCR.

Does it change my files?

No. Fynder reads your files and stores a separate local search index. Highlights appear in its preview.

Do I need an internet connection?

You need internet access to download the app, Ollama and the model. Once those are installed, document search with a local model runs on your computer.
