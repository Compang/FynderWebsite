Built around your documents

Local means
it stays with you.

With a local Ollama model, document text is processed on your computer instead of being sent to an outside AI service.

The website’s contact form is separate from the desktop app and uses an external email-delivery service.

  • PDF, Word & Excel
    Alongside text, Markdown, CSV, JSON, logs and saved HTML.
  • Keyword search without AI
    Enable Ollama embeddings when you want searches by meaning.
  • An index that stays current
    While open, Fynder checks for changed files every minute.

Current limits: 30 MB per file. Scanned PDFs need OCR, which is not included.
